Do you plan both small and large events? +

Yes. Wonderland Sweets works with the vision behind each celebration, whether it is an intimate gathering or a larger event. Tell us your occasion and the support you need.

Can you create a backdrop that matches my theme? +

Custom backdrops and balloon styling are part of our services. Share your colors, theme, ideas and date so we can discuss what is possible for your event.

Do you offer tables, chairs, linens and tents? +

We offer event rentals including tables, chairs, tablecloths and select tents. Availability depends on the event details, so please contact us with the date and items you need.

Can you style a dessert or sweets table? +

Yes. Dessert and sweets tables are a core part of Wonderland Sweets. Tell us your celebration theme and the type of presentation you have in mind.

Where is Wonderland Sweets located? +

We are based in Miami, Florida 33155. Share your event location when you reach out so we can discuss service availability for your area.

Does pricing depend on my event? +

Each celebration has different needs. Send us the event type, date, location and services you are interested in so we can discuss an appropriate custom quote.

How do I get a quote? +

Use the form below to tell us your occasion, preferred date, location and services. It prepares a WhatsApp message you can review and send directly to our contact number.
